# Redundancy review

Required during maintenance and optimization discovery. Compare meaning and applicability, not matching text alone. Use the calling workflow's eligible discovered set and relevant pointers; respect privacy/generated boundaries rather than crawling links.

General maintenance includes this even when optimization is declined. Focused/trace scope stays relevant to the symptom/check. Coding Balanced compares its representative set; Strict uses broader inspected coverage. State omissions rather than implying workspace-wide completeness.

## Procedure

1. **Group repeated meaning:** exact copies, paraphrases, overlapping clauses, copied steps, and repeated pointers within and across files. Cite paths/headings or line ranges.
2. **Check applicability:** required action, trigger, scope, precedence, exceptions, and qualifiers. Same text under different scopes may be necessary; pointers can serve distinct branches/independent entry points. A thin reference isn't a duplicate of its target's detail.
3. **Classify:** use the table. For partial overlap, separate shared clauses from each unique requirement. Resolve conflicting authority with a user decision, not the shortest wording.
4. **Test the home:** a consolidation candidate needs a supported canonical home reachable from every affected task route/independent directory. Preserve unique constraints and every distinct pointer condition; ask or leave unresolved when ownership/reachability is unclear.

| Disposition | Meaning / action |
| --- | --- |
| **Consolidation candidate** | Same meaning/applicability without reason for repetition; propose smallest merge/removal/thin pointer to a reachable home. |
| **Partial overlap** | Shared meaning with distinct qualifiers/actions; consolidate shared clauses only. |
| **Retain intentionally** | Safety, review, local boundaries, compatibility, independent entry points, or explicit preservation needs repetition; explain why. |
| **Uncertain or conflicting** | Meaning, authority, applicability, or preservation unresolved; name the decision rather than proposing deletion. |

Keep pointer targets and every distinct read condition. Keep always-needed prerequisites reachable; local rules need a reachable destination from their own entry point.

## Reporting and completion

Each group reports locations, meaning/target, disposition/evidence, retained unique constraints, smallest safe action, and proposed home or unresolved decision. Keep sensitive content out of examples/findings.

In maintenance, harmless removable repetition is a **Suggestion**; behavior/safety/routing divergence is a **Warning**, with likely cause and repair tier. Repetition alone isn't hard failure. Retained/uncertain groups are coverage notes, not removable duplicates.

In optimization, pointer findings belong in **Context pointers**, ownership/pruning in **Canonical home** or **Pruning** within the existing five-row audit. Refer to one finding per group across rows; check edits against the preservation ledger.

Finish when the inspected set has been compared within and across files, each candidate has a disposition, and coverage limits are stated. No findings means only that inspected scope; uninspected areas aren't Pass. This is qualitative review, not equivalence proof or measured token savings.

Review authorizes no edits. Use the shared final-direction/sign-off contract for repairs, then verify constraints and reachable pointers from every affected entry point.
