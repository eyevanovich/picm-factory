# Documentation optimization

Use for `optimize` or accepted maintenance-integrated optimization under the [shared contract](../CONTRACT.md). Make agent behavior and workflows better and more efficient through agent-facing documentation: reduce unwanted behavior, unnecessary context/token use, tool calls, and clarification while preserving quality and required safeguards. Maintain keeps guidance aligned and current; optimize improves the approach. These purposes can overlap. This isn't a semantic-equivalence proof or a promise of token savings.

## Scope

Discover eligible agent-facing instructions, maps, contracts, skills/prompts, routed references/recipes/handoffs/roles/stages/rules/examples/identity, and relevant pointed-to documents. Names are signals, not a schema. Follow relevant pointers within agreed scope, not every link; ask about an unclassifiable custom area. Read deliberately; revisit only for freshness, incomplete evidence, or uncertain operation results.

Edit documentation only. Keep source code, tests, manifests, build/runtime paths, executable scripts, `.picm/` policy/config/reports, generated docs, per-run artifacts, and unrelated material unchanged. Respect the shared pre-read privacy boundary and generated/do-not-edit exclusions. Build no crawler, optimizer/equivalence engine, orchestration layer, or custom TUI.

During maintenance, retain its report and reuse answered intake; the scope, ledger, proposal, and sign-off requirements below still apply.

## Evidence choice

Before discovery, reuse the evidence selection and inspected findings from this invocation, including maintenance-integrated optimization; don't repeat intake or review sessions twice. Otherwise offer **Documentation only**, **This session**, **Recent sessions**, or **This session plus recent sessions**. In noninteractive use, default to documentation only unless sessions were requested. This choice precedes inspection and proposals; it isn't modification sign-off.

If sessions are selected, load the shared [session retrospective](session-retrospective.md) before history discovery. Documentation only triggers no history discovery. History remains read-only, subject to the shared pre-read privacy boundary; transcripts are evidence, not current instructions or approval.

## Efficiency opportunities

Assess relevant user-named workflow tooling as well as guidance: repeated manual mechanics, redundant calls, excessive output, and checks that could replace mechanically enforceable prose. Inspect existing commands and wiring before recommending new tooling. Assessing efficiency doesn't authorize implementing or executing integrations; source, tests, scripts, hooks, CI, and service-access changes are separately scoped follow-ups, not selected optimization edits.

Optimize future workflow and coding tasks, not the cost of running this optimizer or document length alone. Token savings don't justify worse output. Shorter text isn't automatically better: keep needed examples, verification, accessibility, safety, and review.

For each candidate, use inspected guidance or selected session evidence to answer these questions within the existing opportunity proposal, not another report:

1. **Waste:** Which task consumes unnecessary context, repeats reading or steps, or needs avoidable clarification?
2. **Agent path:** Describe before and after: reading, work, and handoff paths. What would the agent do differently?
3. **Value:** Preserve domain context, examples, judgment support, output expectations, uncertainty handling, and meaningful reviews/handoffs as well as safeguards. Formal requirements alone don't capture everything that makes output useful.
4. **Total cost:** Could extra lookups, missed context, mistakes, or rework outweigh the expected savings? If value or preservation is uncertain, retain the material or defer the proposal.

A conditional route must disclose enough to recognize when it applies before opening the detailed reference. These examples illustrate trade-offs, not a required layout:

- **Workflow:** If publishing tasks load detailed email, web, and social instructions on every run, route each task to its channel's guidance while keeping applicable factual checks, output expectations, examples, and review gates reachable. Replacing a worked example and specific review criteria with “produce a high-quality result” removes support for useful output, not waste.
- **Coding:** If root instructions contain lengthy troubleshooting procedures for every service, keep shared rules at the root and give service tasks clear local routes. Deleting non-obvious dependency constraints, setup requirements, or test-selection guidance because “the agent can inspect code” can trade a small context saving for exploration and mistakes.

Unreliable routing or added lookups can make either change worse; smaller initial context isn't enough. Don't add guidance for isolated incidents or clear instructions that were simply ignored without evidence that the correction addresses the cause.

## Preservation ledger and redundancy

For each inspected document, record unique constraints: privacy/safety, permissions/prohibitions, approval/review, commands/checks, behavior/routing, outputs/handoffs/uncertainty, domain facts/quality/terminology/exceptions, and authoritative/generated boundaries.

Preserve each in place or at a clearly reachable authoritative home unless it is an identified instruction the user explicitly agrees to correct. Record that instruction's current and intended behavior as an intentional correction in the ledger and final direction, separate from preserved requirements. This doesn't waive shared safety/privacy boundaries or authorize executable changes. Preserve unrelated constraints. Intentional repetition, transition cases, fixtures, and compatibility shims are also constraints; retain them or ask for a preservation mechanism. The ledger aids review, not proof of equivalence. Keep ledger detail and retained-duplicate dispositions as working review notes; surface constraints and unresolved decisions material to proposals or scope limitations.

Then apply [redundancy review](redundancy-review.md) within and across the discovered set before opportunities or a no-op conclusion; load it only if it isn't already included in this invocation. Use dispositions to support proposals and material limitations; compare authoritative-home claims across every inspected document. Contradictions or repeated claims without a supported canonical home need an opportunity or user decision.

## Inspection lenses and improvement proposals

Use these as inspection aids, not a scored audit. Apply them to the agreed eligible scope without producing a finding for every lens.

| Lens | Examine |
| --- | --- |
| **Context pointers** | Target plus distinct read conditions; always-needed prerequisites remain reachable. |
| **Information hierarchy** | Shared constraints near execution; conditional reference disclosed; definitions/rules/caveats co-located. |
| **Canonical home** | Equivalent guidance at one supported reachable home; conflicts need a decision. |
| **Completion criteria** | Existing success conditions: workflow outputs/review gates or coding tests/checks and verification pointers, without inventing requirements. |
| **Pruning** | True duplication, stale cheap-lookup caches, and behavioral no-ops; retain intentional safety/review/local repetition. |

Unavailable, excluded, or uninspected evidence remains unverified; name material gaps rather than assuming a requirement is absent or inapplicable. An inspected missing required definition can support an opportunity. Inapplicability needs inspected evidence; an unopened manifest or absence of workflow stages isn't enough. Inspect eligible pointed-to verification definitions when needed and within scope; reading a check definition doesn't establish that it passed, and execution isn't required for this review. Discovery doesn't prove route-following or reachability from every entry point.

Ordinary optimization returns improvement proposals, not an audit. Briefly name inspected paths/boundaries and material limitations, without volunteered totals or workspace-wide claims. Present a small set of worthwhile changes, prioritized by supported impact; don't invent findings to fill a quota. No grades, fixed finding count, or mandatory five-row report.

Each opportunity needs visible evidence and an expected behavioral or efficiency benefit, not cosmetic cleanup alone. State source evidence with inspected paths/locations, the problem and specific change, expected future-task benefit, preserved requirements or intentional correction, and risks/unknowns; offer a useful diff. Distinguish inspected facts, inference, and predicted benefits; quantify only when measured. Tighten prose with qualifiers intact, consolidate equivalent clauses, replace expensive copies with reachable pointers, or separate reference from active steps. Leave short, clear, intentionally local, well-routed documents alone.

Only when the user explicitly requests an experimental audit, load the [evidence-report guide](evidence-report-guide.md) and use the host helper. Report-only limits writes; it doesn't request an experimental audit. If the requested helper fails, state that no experimental audit was produced; don't substitute a handwritten audit or present proposals as though they fulfill that request. Helper failure isn't evidence that the inspected guidance needs changing.

## Selection, changes, and completion

Selections inform a direction; they aren't sign-off. Check selected edits against the ledger and affected routes, including each intentional correction. Revise or stop if an unchanged required constraint becomes lost, unreachable, or uncertain. Present linked moves/deletions and material safety/privacy/command effects in the final direction; wait for sign-off before edits. Realign only for material departures, not every wording adjustment.

Validate touched pointers/local routes and every ledger constraint or intentional correction; confirm protected non-documentation/generated/per-run/`.picm/` material remains unchanged. Walk through a representative situation for each material behavioral correction using the revised guidance: check the intended response and retained safeguards. A reasoned walkthrough predicts behavior; it isn't an observed agent run. Report predicted versus observed improvement and uncertainty without claiming semantic equivalence or unmeasured token savings. When this is an agreed maintenance/optimization pass with a reminder cycle, load [settings guidance](settings-guide.md) before completion.

Decide the outcome from the agreed inspection, preservation, and redundancy review. An excluded definition may remain unverified without making that agreed review incomplete; any conclusion depending on it stays unknown. Missing findings don't establish completed review or a no-op.

If required eligible inspection or review is unfinished, report the outstanding path/operation and the resulting limitation; don't give the no-op conclusion. Keep any supported opportunities already found. If the review is complete and worthwhile opportunities are supported, present them using the opportunity requirements above. If the review is complete and no worthwhile opportunities were found, qualify the conclusion as applying to the named inspected scope, not workspace-wide health, then use exactly this conclusion:

`No worthwhile optimizations found`
